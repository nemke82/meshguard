Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

MeshGuard is an open-source, peer-to-peer messenger for Meshtastic radios. It has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. This policy covers the MeshGuard apps for Android, Linux and macOS, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. The MeshGuard developers do not operate any server that receives your messages, contacts, location or usage data. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries, and it does not share data with third parties.

What the app stores on your device

  • Saved radios — the name, node number and Bluetooth address (or network address / USB port) of radios you connected to, so MeshGuard can list them and reconnect automatically. You can forget a radio at any time.
  • Paired peers — each peer's radio name, Meshtastic node number and protocol version.
  • Chat keys — the encryption key for each paired chat, so you don't have to pair again. Each key is encrypted with a random device key. On Android that device key is itself encrypted by a key in the Android Keystore, which never leaves the Keystore and is held in secure hardware where the phone provides it; on Linux and macOS it is kept in your operating system's keyring (Secret Service, Keychain). Removing a peer deletes its key.
  • Message history — the messages, check-ins and SOS alerts you send and receive, with their time and delivery status, so a conversation is still there after you close the app, and unsent messages can be retried when your radio is back. Message text and contact names are encrypted on the device with a key derived from the same device key; the time, node numbers and delivery status are stored unencrypted so the app can sort and retry messages. History is kept until you delete the conversation. It is never uploaded. If the device has no Keystore or keyring available, history is kept in memory only while the app runs.
  • Duplicate-protection records — message IDs and timestamps used to detect replayed or relayed copies. They never contain message text.
  • Notification settings, including a custom sound if you choose one.

Your passphrase is never saved to storage and never transmitted; it is used only to derive the encryption key. MeshGuard opts out of Android cloud backup, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device. On desktop, removing the app's data folder removes the history; the device key stays in your keyring until you delete it there.

Location

MeshGuard does not use your phone's location. When you send a check-in with location, share your location or send an SOS, MeshGuard attaches the last position reported by your Meshtastic radio — from its GPS, or a fixed position set on the radio — with its accuracy and when it was measured. It is encrypted like a message and sent only to the contacts you chose; SOS goes to all your paired contacts. Received positions are shown in the chat; the "Map" link opens openstreetmap.org in your browser only when you tap it.

What leaves your device

Messages are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) and sent through your own Meshtastic radio, over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi/TCP or USB, to the LoRa mesh. Other mesh nodes relay the encrypted packets; only the contact who holds the key for your conversation (from the QR code you scanned together, or from your shared passphrase) can read them. Like any radio transmission, Meshtastic packets reveal metadata such as node numbers, signal strength and timing to radios within range.

Permissions (Android)

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan and connect) — to find and connect to your Meshtastic radio. Scanning is declared as not used for location.
  • Location — requested only on Android 11 and older, where the system requires it for Bluetooth scanning. MeshGuard does not read or store your location.
  • Notifications — to alert you to incoming messages.
  • Camera — only to scan a pairing QR code when you tap Scan code. No photo or video is stored or sent.
  • Internet — only for finding Meshtastic radios on your local network (mDNS, when you tap Scan Network) and connecting to one over Wi-Fi/TCP. MeshGuard does not contact any other server.

This website

meshguard.net is a static site hosted on Vercel. It uses no cookies or analytics; your theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and user agent).

Children

MeshGuard is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the source repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/nemke82/meshguard/issues.
