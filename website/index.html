At a glance

The short answers

What is MeshGuard?

A free messaging app for Android, Linux and macOS. It connects to a Meshtastic LoRa radio and lets you chat privately with people who use MeshGuard on their own radio.

What hardware do I need?

One Meshtastic radio per person, plus a phone or computer. The project tests with the Seeed SenseCAP T1000-E. See supported hardware.

Does it need mobile service or internet?

No. Messages travel between radios. Your phone talks to its own radio over Bluetooth (or your local Wi-Fi network, if the radio supports it). No SIM card and no internet connection are needed.

How do I pair and send my first message?

Connect each phone to its radio, then meet in person and scan a QR code to pair. Follow the step-by-step guide.

How it works

Your phone, your radio, the mesh

Your phone connects to a compatible Meshtastic radio over Bluetooth. The radio transmits your messages over LoRa, directly or through compatible mesh relay nodes. Another radio delivers the packets to your contact’s MeshGuard app.

  1. Your phoneMeshGuard encrypts the message
  2. Bluetooth
  3. Your Meshtastic radioSends it over LoRa
  4. LoRa radio
  5. Mesh relays (optional)Other nodes may pass it on
  6. LoRa radio
  7. Your contact’s radioReceives the packet
  8. Bluetooth
  9. Your contact’s phoneMeshGuard decrypts it

No internet required

The radios carry the messages. Connecting to a radio over local Wi-Fi is also possible, and that is your home network, not the internet.

Encrypted before it leaves

MeshGuard encrypts each message on your phone, and only your contact’s MeshGuard can read it. Relays only see an encrypted packet.

Range is real-world

How far a message gets depends on terrain, antennas, height, radio settings and which relay nodes are in reach. There is no fixed promise.

Getting started

Get started in four steps

  1. Prepare your radios

    Use two compatible Meshtastic radios. Set the same region, modem preset and primary channel on both, in the Meshtastic app.

  2. Install and connect

    Install MeshGuard, allow Bluetooth access, select your radio and complete the Bluetooth connection.

  3. Pair securely

    Meet in person: one phone shows a QR code, the other scans it. This pairs the two MeshGuard users, not the phone and its radio.

  4. Start communicating

    Send messages, watch the delivery ticks, send a quick check-in, and keep an eye on the radio connection.

Features

What MeshGuard does today

Everything below is in the current release. See every feature and its status.

Encrypted conversations

MeshGuard encrypts messages before handing them to the radio network.

Secure offline pairing

Add a trusted contact by scanning a QR code in person. No online account.

Delivery confirmations

One tick when your radio has the message; two ticks when your contact’s MeshGuard confirms it arrived.

Family check-ins

Send “I’m OK” or “On my way” with one tap. Change the wording to whatever your family uses.

SOS and location

Send an urgent message to your paired contacts, with your radio’s GPS position when it has a fix. Not a substitute for emergency services.

Mesh diagnostics

See signal quality, battery and hop count for each node, and run a path test to see how a message travels.
