FAQ

Questions and troubleshooting

Quick answers about connecting, pairing, delivery, range, privacy and GPS. Still stuck? Open an issue on GitHub.

Bluetooth and connecting

MeshGuard does not find my radio.

Turn Bluetooth on and allow MeshGuard’s nearby-devices permission (location on Android 11 and older). Close the Meshtastic app or disconnect it there: a radio connects to one app at a time. Make sure the radio is charged and close by, then tap Scan for Devices again.

What is the Bluetooth PIN?

Radios without a screen, like the SenseCAP T1000-E, use a fixed PIN, 123456 by default. Radios with a screen show a PIN on the display when you connect. This PIN only links your phone to your own radio; it has nothing to do with your encrypted chats.

Connecting takes a long time.

On first connect, the radio sends MeshGuard its list of known nodes. On a busy mesh with hundreds of nodes that can take up to a minute. Later reconnects happen on their own.

Can I connect without Bluetooth?

Yes. A radio with Wi-Fi can be reached over your local network (the WiFi tab), and on Linux and macOS over a USB cable. Neither needs internet access.

Channels and radio settings

Which radio settings must match?

Both radios need the same LoRa region, the same modem preset and the same primary channel (name and key). Set these in the official Meshtastic app. MeshGuard sends on the primary channel.

Should I use a private channel?

It is optional. MeshGuard encrypts every message itself, so others on the default channel cannot read it. A private channel also keeps your packets out of other Meshtastic users’ apps. Other nodes with the same radio settings can still relay your packets without being able to decrypt them, depending on their rebroadcast settings.

Pairing

What does pairing actually do?

It creates a secret key that only the two of you hold, for your conversation. With a QR code the key is random; with a passphrase it is derived from the passphrase and both radios. The key is never sent over the air. Pairing is between MeshGuard users, not between a phone and its radio.

Scanning the QR code does not work.

Allow the camera when asked. If the camera cannot be used, MeshGuard offers to type the code shown under the QR instead. The code is valid for 10 minutes; show a new one if it expired.

It says the contact is already paired.

Remove the contact on both phones (the × on the chat card), then pair again.

What is MeshGuard 2.0 mode?

The current message format, with delivery confirmations and replay protection. Leave it on. Turn it off only when pairing with someone who still runs an old MeshGuard version.

Delivery and confirmations

What do ✓ and ✓✓ mean?

⏳ the message is saved and waiting for the radio. ✓ your radio has accepted it. ✓✓ your contact’s MeshGuard received and decrypted it. ⚠ MeshGuard gave up: no confirmation came back after its last attempt, or the message reached the end of its lifetime.

My message stays at one tick.

The confirmation has to come back over the mesh, so it can take a while or not arrive if your contact is out of reach or their app is closed. MeshGuard resends it a few times, with growing pauses, then marks it ⚠ if no confirmation came. Contacts paired with 2.0 mode off never show two ticks.

What happens to messages I write while the radio is away?

They are saved and sent when the radio is connected again, as long as they have not expired.

Range

How far does it reach?

It depends on terrain, height, antennas, radio settings and nearby relay nodes. Open ground with a clear view carries much further than a street between buildings or a radio in a pocket. Test with your own radios where you plan to use them.

Will other Meshtastic nodes relay my messages?

They may. Relaying depends on each node’s settings, the hop limit, the channel and how busy the mesh is. Relays only see an encrypted packet.

Privacy and security

Who can read my messages?

Only your contact’s MeshGuard. Messages are encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM, with a key only the two of you hold. This is separate from the Meshtastic channel key, which everyone on the channel shares.

Is MeshGuard anonymous?

No. Radios in range can see that your radio transmits, its node number, the timing and signal strength. MeshGuard protects what you say, not the fact that you are sending.

What is stored on my phone?

Your saved radios, contacts, chat keys and message history. Chat keys and message text are encrypted with a random device key, which the Android Keystore protects. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. Details in the privacy policy.

GPS and location

Why is there no location in my check-in or SOS?

Location comes from the radio’s GPS, not the phone. The radio needs a current fix: take it outside with a view of the sky and wait a few minutes, and check that GPS is enabled in its Meshtastic settings. Without a fix, MeshGuard sends without a location and tells you.

Does SOS call emergency services?

No. SOS sends an urgent message to your paired MeshGuard contacts only, and only reaches them if the radios can. Call your local emergency number if you can.

App closed and phone locked

Do I get messages when the app is closed?

Not yet. MeshGuard receives messages while it is running. If Android stops it in the background or you close it, messages are not shown until you open it again; a 2.0 sender resends a few times before giving up, so some may still arrive later. Background messaging is in development.

Can I reply from the notification?

Not yet. Replying from a notification is in development and is not part of the current release.
